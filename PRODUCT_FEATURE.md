# AI Autonomous Shopping Agent --- Product Roadmap

## Vision

Build an AI shopping assistant that autonomously discovers, compares,
monitors and purchases products across marketplaces while Mandate Gate
securely authorizes every payment.

## Product Positioning

-   Product: AI Autonomous Shopping Agent
-   Trust Layer: Mandate Gate

## Core User Flow

1.  Search product.
2.  Compare marketplaces.
3.  Select product.
4.  Create Shopping Rule.
5.  Generate signed mandate.
6.  AI monitors prices.
7.  Calculate final checkout cost.
8.  Validate via Mandate Gate.
9.  Execute payment.
10. Generate receipt.
11. Notify user.

## Price Intelligence

Monitor: - Price - Stock - Offers - Coupons - Delivery - Platform fee -
Taxes - Cashback

## Final Checkout Intelligence

Calculate: - Product price - Delivery - Platform fee - Handling fee -
Taxes - Discounts - Coupons - Membership savings

Always compare FINAL checkout amount instead of listed product price.

## Autonomous Purchase

Purchase only when: - Price \<= target - Merchant allowed - Budget
available - Mandate valid - Final checkout within limit
